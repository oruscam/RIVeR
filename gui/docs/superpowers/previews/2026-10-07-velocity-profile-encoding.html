Why it reads as noise today

MarkProblem
Interpolated stretchRecoloured in the theme accent. In dark, accent blue sits next to LSPIV cyan; in Dracula, accent violet sits next to iWave pink. With 80% interpolated, the technique's own hue is visible on one segment only.
Interpolated markerFilled with the accent, so a dot at an estimated station looks like a measured one in another colour.
iWave qualityEncoded twice (pale→saturated and small→large). Pale large triangles read as a different series.
STIV automaticHollow square for the normal case, so almost every STIV marker looks "special".
Rejected iWaveDashed outline at 45% is so faint it reads as a rendering artefact near the axis.

Proposed grammar

SignalEncoding
TechniqueIts own hue and shape (● ■ ▲), everywhere, including interpolated stretches and bands.
Selected technique3.25 px line, full opacity, markers r 3.75, drawn on top. Others: 2.25 px, 60% opacity, markers r 2.75.
Measured stationFilled marker with a thin panel-coloured ring so it stays crisp on the line.
Interpolated stationNo marker; the segments touching it are dotted (or dashed / faded). Same hue.
iWave qualityMarker size only (r 2.5 → 5.5), same hue.
Rejected iWaveHollow marker in the iWave hue at its raw velocity, not connected.
STIV tuned angleThe exception is marked, not the rule: a tuned station gets a thin outer ring; automatic ones are plain filled squares.
