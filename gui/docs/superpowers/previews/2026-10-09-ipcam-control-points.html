Fixed station control points

Proposal for the Control Points step of the fixed station footage. Section 1 is the overlay drawn on the frame, which is the same in every theme. Section 2 is the panel in dark, light and dracula. The background of the frame is a stand-in with the tones of the real scene (dry grass, brown water, concrete), to check the overlay against each of them.

1. On the frame

The ellipse becomes an outline in the shared overlay style: white over a dark halo, no fill. The residual is a haloed line from the pin to the reprojected point, coloured by that point's own error with the grade colours of the pins palette (fixed in every theme, like the pins). The active point shows its label and error. Points left out of the solve keep a small grey pin and no overlay.

2. The panel

Order: import, method, result, then the points table and the map. The result uses the row grammar of the other steps (14px label, tabular value, unit in subdued text) with the mean error one step up and graded with the same badge as Camera Calibration. The two solve methods are an equal choice on a Method switch (All points keeps every ticked point, Optimized drops points to lower the error), and one Solve sits in the footer as in Oblique and UAV. The table drops the pixel x / y columns, which the pin already shows, and gains Error (px): click the header to sort, worst first. Proposed grades, on the frame and in the table alike: good under 5 px, fair from 5 to 10 px, bad over 10 px.
