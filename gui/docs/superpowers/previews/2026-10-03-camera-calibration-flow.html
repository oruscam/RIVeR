Camera calibration: a Prepare screen, then the workspace

Proposal, not shipped. Home opens a quiet Prepare screen built like the footage-type screen. The two-panel workspace only appears once there are photos to solve, and saving a profile ends the way saving a report does. Use the switch above each window to see the Prepare screen's states.

  1. No canvas until there is something to show. Prepare has no canvas or strip. Solve opens the workspace and starts the solve.
  2. Two numbered steps. The order is the instruction: show the board, then import photos. Step 1 says it can be skipped.
  3. Guidance before shooting. The three tips say what the solver later grades: count, viewpoint variety, corner reach.
  4. A drop area RIVeR already has. The same dashed box and button as the footage screen, not a full-screen drop zone.
  5. Too few photos warns, it doesn't block. Under 15 photos shows a fair-grade note; Solve stays available.
  6. Ends like the report. Accent check, what was saved, and two real buttons: Show in folder, Calibrate another.
